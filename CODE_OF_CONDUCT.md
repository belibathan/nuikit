# Code of Conduct

nuikit follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, keep feedback about the work, and no harassment of any kind. Roleplay
servers can be competitive; this repo isn't. No advertising servers or paid resources in issues or discussions.

**Reporting.** Contact the maintainer, [@belibathan](https://github.com/belibathan), privately through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/belibathan/nuikit/security/advisories/new) titled "Conduct". Reports
are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content, lock threads or block accounts that break these rules.
