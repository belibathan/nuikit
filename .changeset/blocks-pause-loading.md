Two more blocks: `npm create nuikit@latest my-pause -- -t pause-menu` (Esc opens it in place of GTA's pause menu, with the game's map, settings and key bindings one click away and a confirm before Disconnect) and `-t loading-screen` (the real load progress phase by phase, tips, links, backgrounds and music, themed through the connection handover). Both are also in the registry (`…/r/pause-menu.json`, `…/r/loading-screen.json`).
