Registry: new `confirm` component (`npx shadcn add https://nuikit.vercel.app/r/confirm.json`), the default look of the `confirm` intent. `Confirm` (a scrim and a centred panel, `role="alertdialog"`) and `ConfirmPanel` (the panel alone): title, body, icon, cancel and confirm labels, primary or destructive, running and acknowledge-only states. Escape cancels through the nuikit surface Escape path, Enter presses the focused action, Tab stays inside, focus returns to the opener. No package changes.
