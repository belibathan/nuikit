The whole kit: one `nuikit.zip` with `[nuikit]` systems (HUD, stress, pause, death, loading screen, chat theme) and `overrides/` for ox_lib, ox_inventory, ox_target, illenium-appearance, Renewed-Banking, mm_radio, qbx_scoreboard, qbx_idcard, qbx_taxijob and qbx_vehiclesales. Pages that replace another resource's UI can opt out of the `nuikit:ready` handshake with `<meta name="nuikit-handshake" content="off">`.
