A garage block: `npm create nuikit@latest my-garage -- -t garage` lists your vehicles at a garage with fuel, engine and body, and takes them out, stores the one you're driving, or releases one from the impound, with a working standalone example (vehicles kept per player in a JSON file) and the lines to change for Qbox, QBCore or ESX. Also in the registry (`…/r/garage.json`) and on `/blocks`.
