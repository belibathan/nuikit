Registry: new `form-field` component (`npx shadcn add https://nuikit.vercel.app/r/form-field.json`), the default look of the `input` intent. `FormField` (label, hint, description, error, required, disabled, inline value, reset) and presets `TextField` (password, multiline, length count), `NumberField` (stepper, min/max, precision), `SelectField` (single or multiple), `CheckboxField` (checkbox or switch), `SliderField` (reset to a value), `DateField` (typed day/month/year with min/max) and `ColorField` (hex and swatches), plus `validateField()`. No package changes.
