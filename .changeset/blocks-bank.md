A bank block: `npm create nuikit@latest my-bank -- -t bank` shows accounts, balances and recent transactions, with deposit, withdraw and transfer checked on the page and again on the server; a standalone example (cash, checking and savings per player) and `Config.money` for a framework's own cash and bank. Also in the registry (`…/r/bank.json`) and on `/blocks`.
