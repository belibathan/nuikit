`notice` slides in from its stack's edge and, through `<Notices>`, back out toward it when it ends while the others glide into the gap (the motion nuikit's own notifications had). `slide={false}` fades in place; `leaving` plays the exit for a queue of your own; `NOTICE_EXIT_MS` is how long to keep it drawn.
