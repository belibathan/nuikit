Hugeicons is the kit's icon set: the `icons` registry item renders Hugeicons under lucide's names, `lucide-react` resolves to it, and the icon stroke defaults to 1.5. Text styles (`nk-text-*`), the flat baseline's Barlow Condensed upright display face, and the minimap at GTA's aspect ship in the same release.
