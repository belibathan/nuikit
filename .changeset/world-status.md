Tokens 1.3 adds `--world-success` and `--world-destructive`: gains and losses drawn on the game or the HUD plate, derived from `--success` / `--destructive` with their lightness raised to 0.7, so a light theme's dark status colours still read on the dark plate. The slab delta (`+2`, `−1` on item notices) uses them.
