Registry: new `item-slot` component (`npx shadcn add https://nuikit.vercel.app/r/item-slot.json`): one square of an inventory, shop, hotbar or crafting grid, with image, name, count, hotkey or stock, price, a durability line, and the drag-and-drop states (empty, active, drop target, dragging, locked). Wells on a panel or the HUD surface for a hotbar; a focusable button with `onClick`. No package changes.
