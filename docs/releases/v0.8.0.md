# 0.8.0

Universal, first cut, and the owner's in-game design. Scripts ask for intents and nuikit draws them; ox_lib,
QBCore and ESX libraries land on those intents; servers can swap how any intent looks; and the screens are
rebuilt the way the owner judged them live on his server.

**Not yet tested in game as a whole.** Each part has a short checklist in its PR; report what breaks.

**Intents** ([intents.md](../intents.md)): `nk.notify`, `nk.menu`, `nk.input`, `nk.confirm`, `nk.progress`,
`nk.prompt`, `nk.target`, `nk.radial`, `nk.challenge`, validated from one schema source, yielding like `lib.*`,
drawn by default renderers in the `nuikit` resource's own frame (one stack, one focus owner).

**Adapters** ([adapters/](../adapters/README.md)):
- ox_lib routes notify, menus, input, alert, progress, text UI and skill check to the shared renderers, falling
  back to drawing them itself when `nuikit` isn't running (`nuikit_ox_route`). Every POST unchanged.
- `[nuikit-qb]`: qb-menu, qb-input, QBCore notify, progressbar, DrawText, qb-radialmenu, qb-target (recording only).
- `[nuikit-esx]`: esx_notify, esx_context, esx_menu_default, esx_menu_dialog, esx_progressbar, esx_textui.
- `[nuikit-standalone]`: okokNotify, mythic_notify. Each pack ships in its framework's zip.

**Renderer packs:** `nuikit_renderer 'notify'` in a resource's fxmanifest replaces that intent's look, themed and
stacked by the runtime; a broken pack falls back and `nuikit doctor` says so. `npm create nuikit -t renderer-pack`.

**Services** ([services.md](../services.md)): `nk.camera` (stacked claims, restore on release, stop and death; the
pause menu uses it) and `nk.controls` (merged control sets, tied to surfaces).

**The owner's design** ([design.md](../design.md)):
- Surfaces: flush sheets when the game is paused or replaced, floating sheets for everything else; rows, not
  cards; sub-pages swap in place. Every shadcn component vendored; `tw-animate-css` in every page.
- Motion: sheets slide in and out, rows stagger, text rolls (`text-roll`), the selection fill and ring glide as one,
  a smooth caret (`smooth-input`).
- HUD: one vital-chip family with the level traced on the chip's outline, pops from the side, rolling location and
  cash. Pause: a flush sheet with the map and every native blip inside, a camera that keeps your view and moves in,
  no studio light.
- ox_lib and ox_target: context and list menus as sheets with one selection indicator, a radial ring, alert
  dialog, target lock-on. ox_inventory, Renewed-Banking, illenium-appearance, qbx_scoreboard, qbx_vehiclesales,
  qbx_taxijob, qbx_idcard and mm_radio as floating sheets (`edge-sheet`).
- A `job-sheet` block (`-t job-sheet`).

**Fixes:** the minimap ring blank after a HUD restart (the `nuikit` resource holds the scaleform); GTA's vehicle
name over the speedometer; `nuikit doctor` classifies the loading screen and chat, prints each contrast finding once,
and hints `refresh` after a manifest change. GPS route outside the ring: candidates behind
`Config.minimap.routeClip` (pick the one that works in game).

**Also:** currency and locale for the whole server (`nuikit_currency`, `nuikit_locale` in `nuikit.cfg`); component
follow-ups; open owner decisions in [`next/decisions.md`](../next/decisions.md).

**Not in yet:** the image resolver's Lua lookups (clothing thumbnails, the car picture in the sales contract):
pages ask for them and fall back silently until they land.

**Install / upgrade:** replace `[nuikit]` (keep `theme.css`), copy `overrides/` over each resource, `exec
@nuikit/nuikit.cfg` in server.cfg; for QBCore/ESX servers, also `ensure` the adapter pack from your zip and stop
the library it replaces (each adapter's README says which). `nuikit doctor` tells you what's left.

## Deprecations moved

`key-hint`, `task-progress` and `nui-toaster` were due to be removed in 0.8.0 but are still in the registry: the site's pages still demo `key-hint`, and the replacements (`key-prompt`; `meter` and `progress-ring`; `notice`) are being adopted across the kit first. Their removal moves to 0.9.0.
