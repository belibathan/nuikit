# v0.7.0 experiment-server handoff: owner → lead

From the owner's Qbox test server, 2026-10-20/21. v0.7.0 was installed, retested and then used as an experiment ground: we built,
the owner judged in game, and we kept what felt premium. This doc is what to take forward. Evidence and the full log are in
`docs/releases/v0.7.0-results.md`, and the patterns are in `docs/releases/v0.7.0-design-patterns.md`.

## 0. The most important change: how agents work

**Kill token- and time-heavy practices in every agent workflow.** Rapid implementation and experimentation is the way forward.
- **No screenshot sweeps.** No identity × resolution matrices, no before/after sheets, no visual "proof" passes. The owner is in the game and
  has the qualitative eye to spot defects faster than any screenshot review. Expect them to.
- **No excessive safety guards.** It's a local build. Back up once and move. Don't ask permission for anything a backup covers.
- **Keep only the cheap checks that prevent silent breakage:** typecheck, the build, and a quick POST-log diff for an override's main flow (its
  protocol must not break). Nothing else gates shipping.
- **Ship, then iterate.** Build, copy into the live server, report the one restart needed, move on. Small commits so a cut-off loses little.
- What it changed in practice: the owner's verdict on the faster cadence was "so much cleaner, better and faster. its what we need right now."
  Early in the session, agents spent most of their time on screenshot sweeps; after we dropped them, a full screen redesign shipped in minutes.

## 1. Adopt shadcn as the foundation

- **Use shadcn's components as the default building blocks everywhere:** Sheet, Dialog, AlertDialog, ScrollArea, Separator, Item, Kbd, Tooltip,
  Progress, Slider, ToggleGroup… "sheets, drawers, scroll areas, separators. i really think we want to be wiring in as much shadcn as
  possible, while also layering in our own quality."
- **Proposal:** vendor **every** available shadcn component (unmodified, as now), then build nuikit's own layer of components on top
  (registry/nuikit): edge-sheet, vital-chip, text-roll, smooth-input, image resolver. Authors compose; they don't hand-roll.
- **`tw-animate-css` must be in every page.** The pause menu's Sheet didn't animate at all because the page never loaded it. Make it part of
  the template or index.css so this can't happen again.
- **Drawer (vaul 1.1.2) is modal underneath even with `modal={false}`.** It left `body { pointer-events: none }` and broke inventory drag and drop.
  It's vendored but unused; trays use a bottom Sheet. Revisit when vaul changes.

## 2. Surfaces: full bleed vs container (the owner's philosophy)

- **One family:** every window is a shadcn Sheet. "the context as built on that shadcn model is performing perfectly … that should generally
  be the basis for a lot of floating context windows and systems."
- **Flush (full bleed):** the game is paused or replaced, one system screen at a time (pause, the full map, character select). The backdrop touches
  the screen edges and the content sits in an inner column ("full height, with the backdrop touch screen edges but content within main container").
- **Floating (container), the default:** the game keeps running behind it. Inventory, banking, clothing, shops, job menus, context and
  list menus, dialogs. Inset `--nk-edge` (1.5rem), border all round, the identity's corners, shadow. "it may not always be appropriate for
  everything to touch screen edge … it doesnt feel right for the bottom bar to touch bottom of the screen." Trays float too.
- **Inside:** rows, not cards. 48px rows, hairlines, one ScrollArea column with a gutter. **Sub-pages swap in place** inside the same sheet
  with a back row; no extra cards. "simple design systems that are compact intuitive and arent over abundant in square card geometry."
- Open question for systemizing: should job menus get one shared "job sheet" template, so resources never design their own frame?

## 3. Motion and dynamism across the whole identity

The owner rated these the highest of anything in the session. Make motion a first-class, out-of-the-box nuikit feature.
- **Entrances:** sheets slide in from their edge, and rows stagger in after them.
- **Exits:** sheets slide out on close (nuikit-core `<Surface>` keeps the surface drawn up to 250ms; `useSurfaceOpen()`). Input is released at once, so
  the game never feels stuck.
- **Text roll** (`TextRoll` / `NumberRoll`) for every value that changes in place (location, money, counts, labels). "the text rotation
  mechanics are phenomenal … so good and so clean." Rule learned the hard way: **rolls are vertical only; alignment is resolved per layer; width
  changes move neighbours, never the rolling text.** Before that rule, centred text slid diagonally and jolted at the end.
- **Synchronized layers:** a selection indicator is one element. The fill and the ring glide together; rows never paint their own selected background,
  and dividers sit under it. "the foreground fill … isnt moving with it so its a bit disjointed."
- **Smooth caret and typing** (`SmoothInput`) on every text field.
- Everything obeys `/nuikit motion off` (instant) and reduced motion (a fade of 100ms or less).

## 4. What the owner deemed premium (keep and extend)

- The pause menu as a flush Sheet with the map inside the same sheet ("pretty amazing"), and the inventory as floating sheets ("inventory rocks",
  "phenomenal … its insane that looks literally amazing").
- The minimap with GTA's real aspect inside the ring ("actually phenomenal"), the bigger ring, and the radar always on.
- Vitals as one chip family, with the level traced along the chip's own outline in each identity's shape; no labelled special cases ("we want to
  streamline"). Pops grow out of the side of their own chip so they never collide.
- The pause map showing every native blip, with filters and click-to-waypoint: "we want our own menu system to be completely replacement to the
  native systems."
- **Subtlety over spectacle:**
  - **The camera:** we killed the forced spin and the swing round to the face. The focus camera keeps the player's current view and just moves in
    ("not force rotation, that could cause some motion sickness").
  - **The light:** we killed the studio light on the pause and inventory ("immersion breaking"). Lighting presets remain an idea for an
    `nk.light` utility.
- **Universal glue:** connect to the conventions servers already use: clothing image packs, item images, vehicle images, popular resources.
  Configurable templates and silent fallbacks, never a nuikit-only format. "we want to be a universal glue, not one only for certain surfaces."

## 5. Iconography and HUD design systems (explored, partly shelved)

- **The VitalChip anatomy is the keeper:** Root, Shape, Level, Icon, Label, Pop. One SVG path per shape drives the border, clip, outline trace, liquid
  fill and label anchors, and theme tokens pick the variants (`--nk-chip-shape`, `--nk-level-style`, `--nk-label-attach`, `--nk-vital-icon`).
  The owner wants this organizing value kept.
- **Shelved:** the variant looks (diamond, liquid fill, tag labels) and making comms bigger and first in the row. The owner's verdict: "the general aesthetic is
  weak … needs to be larger … labels should be coming out the side." Revisit with stronger craft, not as default.
- Voice and radio are the emphasis of FiveM roleplay; vitals are temporary self-management signals that surface on change.
- Weapon and item icons: use the inventory's own item images through the shared image resolver (in progress) rather than nuikit glyphs.

## 6. Bugs found and fixed (take these into the repo)

- **Empty minimap ring after any HUD restart or start.** Root cause: the HUD's `RequestScaleformMovie('minimap')`. FiveM frees it on stop, and that unloads
  GTA's radar movie. Fix: the `nuikit` resource holds the scaleform and the mask texture for the session (`nuikit:minimap:bars`, `nuikit:minimap:mask`).
  Verified in game. It's a **local Lua patch**, not in any branch: `bench/results/070/patches/local-lua-patches.diff`.
- Vehicle name over the speedo: the hide loop now also checks `IsPedInAnyVehicle` and polls every 50ms on foot (same diff).
- Theme in overrides (F4), ammo with ox_inventory (F1), first-person spin (F5), radar restore on stop (F3) and aspect (note A) all pass in game on v0.7.0.
- **Still open:** the GPS route draws outside the ring (it ignores the mask; clip type 0 is worse). The doctor misclassifies `nuikit_loading`, omits
  `nuikit_chat` and prints the contrast issue twice. A `refresh` is needed after an fxmanifest file-list change (bit us once).
